Privacy Policy for NFC Bankroll

Effective Date: September 15, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to NFC Bankroll ("we," "our," "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This application is designed to modernize classic board games by providing a cashless transaction system.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application (the "App"). The core principle of our App is privacy by design. The App works completely offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be crystal clear about what we do not do. Our App does not collect, store, or transmit any Personal Identifiable Information (PII). This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your device's unique identifiers (like IMEI or Advertising ID).
  • Your location data.
  • Your contacts, photos, or other files on your device.
  • Any usage analytics or crash reports.

Since the App is fully offline, it is technically incapable of sending any data from your device to us or any third party.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the core gameplay experience, the App stores a minimal amount of game-related data exclusively on your device's local storage. This data includes:

  • Player any random nicknames that you enter for a game session.
  • Current in-game account fake like same monopony coins balances for each player.
  • Game session history managed in locally.

This information is created by you during gameplay and is never transmitted off your device. It is essential for the App's function and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the App.

How We Use Information

The locally stored game data is used solely to operate the features of the App, such as displaying player balances and facilitating in-game NFC transactions between players. We do not use this information for any other purpose.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share or sell any data with third parties because we do not collect any personal data to begin with. All game information remains on your device.

Data Security

We take the security of your information seriously. The App's primary security feature is its offline nature. Since no data ever leaves your device via the internet, the risk of unauthorized online access is eliminated. The security of the data stored locally is dependent on the overall security of your mobile device and its operating system.

Children's Privacy

Our App is designed for a general audience and is suitable for families and players of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We take children's privacy very seriously.

The core design of our App ensures a safe environment. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as a real name, email address, or location) from any user, regardless of their age. The app functions entirely offline, meaning no data is ever transmitted from your device.

Any information entered into the app, such as player nicknames, is used only for the duration of the game session and is stored locally on your device. Because we do not collect personal information as defined by the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other similar regulations, our App provides a safe and private experience for all users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or on our app store listing. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
